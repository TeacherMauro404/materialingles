2Present Simple vs Present Continuous

5FIRST CONDITIONAL, Opinions & Preferences

First conditional: situación real o posible en el futuro

If + present simplewill / won't + verbo

If you study,you will pass the test.
If I don't hurry,I will miss the bus.

El orden puede cambiar: We'll stay at home if it rains (sin coma).

Ojo: nunca "will" después de if. ✗ If it will rain…

Giving opinions

I think (that)… · In my opinion,… · I believe… · I don't think… · I agree / I disagree · You're right · That's a good point, but…

Preferences

  • I prefer football to basketball.
  • I prefer watching series to reading.
  • I'd rather stay at home. (= I would rather + verbo)
  • Would you like to come? — I'd love to!

11Real-Life Communication — Peticiones, sugerencias y compras

Polite requests

  • Can I borrow your pen?
  • Could you help me, please? (más formal)
  • Sure! / Of course. / Sorry, I can't.

Suggestions

  • Let's go to the beach!
  • Why don't we watch a film?
  • How about going to the market?
  • Shall we start? — Good idea!

Shopping & money

  • How much is this T-shirt? — It's 8,000 pesos.
  • How much are these shoes?
  • Can I try it on? — The fitting room is over there.
  • Do you have it in a smaller size?
  • I'll take it. / Can I pay by card?

At the doctor / At a restaurant

  • What's the matter? — I have a headache / a cold / a fever.
  • You should rest and drink water.
  • Can I see the menu, please?
  • I'd like a sandwich and a juice, please.
  • Can we have the bill, please?

12Quick Check — Practica lo esencial

A. Present simple or present continuous?

  1. My mother (work) at the hospital.
  2. Shh! The baby (sleep) now.
  3. I (not / like) running.
  4. Look! They (swim) with dolphins.
  5. He (usually / play) the guitar at night.

B. Complete with will or going to.

  1. I've decided. I study engineering.
  2. It's cold. — I close the window.
  3. I think people live on Mars.
  4. If you study, you (pass) the test.
  5. If it (rain), we won't go to the beach.

C. Write the verb in past simple or past continuous.

  1. Yesterday I (go) to the port with my dad.
  2. We (see) a big ship.
  3. She (not / study) for the test.
  4. you (call) Ana last night?
  5. I (walk) home when it started to rain.
  6. The weather (be) terrible last weekend.

D. Comparatives, superlatives & present perfect.

  1. A whale is (big) than a dolphin.
  2. This is (good) day of my life!
  3. Maths is (difficult) than Art.
  4. you ever (be) to Chiloé?
  5. She has (never / eat) sushi.

E. Writing: an unforgettable day (60–80 words)

Usa past simple, past continuous y al menos 4 conectores (first, then, suddenly, after that, finally, because…).

Solucionario: A) 1 works · 2 is sleeping · 3 don't like · 4 are swimming · 5 usually plays — B) 1 am going to · 2 'll (will) · 3 will · 4 will pass · 5 rains — C) 1 went · 2 saw · 3 didn't study · 4 Did / call · 5 was walking · 6 was — D) 1 bigger · 2 the best · 3 more difficult · 4 Have / been · 5 never eaten
