3Subject Pronouns & VERB TO BE — Pronombres y verbo "ser / estar"

El verb to be significa ser o estar. Se usa para decir nombre, edad, nacionalidad, profesión, sentimientos y ubicación. Es el verbo más importante del año.

PronombreAfirmativoContracciónNegativo
I yoI amI'mI'm not
You túyou areyou'reyou aren't
He élhe ishe'she isn't
She ellashe isshe'sshe isn't
It eso (cosa/animal)it isit'sit isn't
We nosotroswe arewe'rewe aren't
You ustedesyou areyou'reyou aren't
They ellos/asthey arethey'rethey aren't

Preguntas: el verbo va primero

PreguntaRespuesta corta
Am I late?Yes, you are. / No, you aren't.
Are you a student?Yes, I am. / No, I'm not.
Is he Chilean?Yes, he is. / No, he isn't.
Is it a ship?Yes, it is. / No, it isn't.
Are they at school?Yes, they are. / No, they aren't.

Ejemplos

  • I am a student at IMV. — She is 15 years old.
  • We are from Valparaíso. — The port is big.
  • They aren't sailors. — Is the boat in the port?
Error típico: ✗ I have 15 years. → ✓ I am 15 years old.

7PRESENT SIMPLE — Rutinas y hábitos

Se usa para rutinas, hábitos y hechos. Ejemplo: I go to school every day. Con he / she / it el verbo lleva -s.

Afirmativo
I / You / We / Theywork
He / She / Itworks
I wake up at 7. — She works at the port.
Negativo
I / You / We / Theydon't work
He / She / Itdoesn't work
He doesn't play football. (¡sin -s!)
Pregunta y respuesta corta
Do you work?Yes, I do. / No, I don't.
Does he work?Yes, he does. / No, he doesn't.
What time do you get up?
Regla 3ª persona (he/she/it)Ejemplo
La mayoría: + -splay → plays, eat → eats
Termina en -s, -sh, -ch, -x, -o: + -eswatch → watches, go → goes
Consonante + y: -iesstudy → studies, fly → flies
Irregularhave → has

12Quick Check — Practica lo esencial

A. Complete with am / is / are (+ o −).

  1. I a student at IMV.
  2. My brother 18 years old.
  3. We from Valparaíso.
  4. The ship in the port. (−)
  5. you Chilean? — Yes, I .
  6. They sailors. They are students. (−)

B. Write the verb in present simple.

  1. She (watch) TV every night.
  2. My father (work) at the port.
  3. I (not / like) Mondays.
  4. he (study) English? — No, he doesn't.
  5. Tomás (have) breakfast at 7.
  6. We (never / go) to school on Sunday.

C. Choose: There is / There are / Is there / Are there.

  1. a lighthouse near the beach.
  2. some boats in the harbour.
  3. a pharmacy in your street?
  4. any parks in your town?

D. Write the question word.

  1. is your birthday? — In June.
  2. do you live? — In Playa Ancha.
  3. is your teacher? — Mr. Mau.

E. About you: answer in complete sentences.

  1. What's your name and how old are you?
  2. Where are you from?
  3. Describe a person in your family.
  4. What do you usually do in the morning?
  5. What can you do? What can't you do?
Solucionario: A) 1 am · 2 is · 3 are · 4 isn't · 5 Are / am · 6 aren't — B) 1 watches · 2 works · 3 don't like · 4 Does / study · 5 has · 6 never go — C) 1 There is · 2 There are · 3 Is there · 4 Are there — D) 1 When · 2 Where · 3 Who

  • Presentarme y dar información personal (nombre, edad, origen).
  • Usar el verb to be en afirmativo, negativo y pregunta.

  • Describir personas con have/has y adjetivos.

  • Dar y pedir direcciones simples.
  • Decir lo que puedo y no puedo hacer con can.
